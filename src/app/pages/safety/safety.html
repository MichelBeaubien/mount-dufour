Share the Mountain

Safety starts with every one of us.

Skiing and snowboarding involve risks. Being aware of your surroundings, staying in control and respecting others helps everyone enjoy the mountain safely.

Before heading out, take a moment to understand the Alpine Responsibility Code and choose terrain that matches your ability.

Stay Aware Stay in control. Respect the mountain.
01

Stay in Control

Always stay in control. You must be able to stop or avoid other people or objects.

02

People Ahead Have the Right of Way

People ahead of you have the right of way. It is your responsibility to avoid them.

03

Keep the Trail Clear

Do not stop where you obstruct a trail or are not visible from above.

04

Look Uphill

Before starting downhill or entering a trail, look uphill and yield to others.

05

Prevent Runaway Equipment

If you are involved in or witness a collision or accident, remain at the scene and identify yourself to ski patrol.

06

Observe Signs & Warnings

Obey all posted signs and warnings. Keep off closed trails and out of closed areas.

07

Use Lifts Safely

You must have sufficient physical dexterity, ability and knowledge to safely load, ride and unload lifts.

08

Know Your Ability

Know how to use the lifts safely. If you are unfamiliar with a lift, ask an attendant for help.

Know the Code. It is your responsibility to be familiar with and follow the Alpine Responsibility Code while using the mountain.

On the Mountain

Make smart choices before every run.

Conditions, visibility and terrain can change throughout the day. Pay attention to what’s happening around you and ski or ride within your ability.

Need Assistance?

Report accidents and hazards.
