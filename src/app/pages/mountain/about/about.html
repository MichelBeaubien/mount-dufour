About Mount Dufour

Big winter experiences, without the big-resort attitude.

Mount Dufour gives skiers and snowboarders a challenging Northern Ontario mountain experience while staying approachable, affordable and local.

From beginner terrain and lessons to steeper runs, a terrain park, rentals and a full-service chalet, Mount Dufour gives Elliot Lake a complete ski-area experience without having to leave the city.

Community Powered

More than 50 years of skiing together.

Mount Dufour is a professionally operated non-profit ski area that has served local residents and visitors for more than five decades.

The hill has grown through the support of volunteers, members, guests, staff and local businesses who continue to help keep skiing accessible in Elliot Lake.
